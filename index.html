Resources & Training

Fast answers so you can keep working without waiting for management on routine questions.

Sales & Customer Service
  • Ask what color/style they actually wear before pitching.
  • Explain the current Nocturne price clearly.
  • Use Product Knowledge for sizes and current inventory.
  • Do not pressure customers or make promises that are not in the Hub.
  • Log real sales in Commission Center.
Social Media Standards
  • Clear camera quality — no blurry uploads.
  • Check spelling, tags, links, and product information before posting.
  • Use approved Photo Collection assets.
  • Prioritize useful engagement over spam comments or DMs.
  • Send required proof before closing.
Customer Questions
  • Use Product Knowledge for fabric, fit, sizes, colors, price, and shipping.
  • If you do not know an answer, tell the customer you will verify it.
  • Do not change policies, pricing, inventory, or order information.
  • Escalate order problems or complaints to management.
Commission Rules
  • Commission is 10% of qualifying customer spend recorded in the Hub.
  • Enter customer, items, colors, sizes, total spend, date, and time accurately.
  • Manager verification determines whether a report is approved.
  • Never create duplicate or fake sale reports.
Proof of Work
  • Use screenshots when a task calls for visual proof.
  • Keep customer/private information out of public social posts.
  • Closing reports should explain what happened, not just say “done.”
  • Use Shift Handoff for anything the next person needs to know.
When to Contact the Manager
  • Account/sign-in or platform verification problem.
  • Customer complaint, refund/exchange issue, or order discrepancy.
  • Inventory/price mismatch.
  • Schedule issue or unexpected overtime.
  • Anything that could affect the brand publicly.
